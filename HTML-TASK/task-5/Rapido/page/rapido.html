About Rapido

Rapido is a bike taxi service that provides quick and affordable transportation. It allows users to book bike rides using a mobile app, making travel easier in busy cities.

Advantages

  • Low-cost transportation.
  • Fast travel in heavy traffic.
  • Easy online booking.
  • Available in many cities.
  • Time-saving for short trips.

Disadvantages

  • Not available everywhere.
  • Limited luggage space.
  • Service depends on rider availability.
  • Not comfortable in bad weather.
  • Not ideal for long-distance travel.
