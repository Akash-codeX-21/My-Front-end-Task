ABOUT

Anime is a broad medium of Japanese animation that isn't about one single thing. However, most anime focuses on overarching themes of personal growth, resilience, friendship, and overcoming adversity. Stories often feature complex, serialized plots where characters go from underdogs to heroes, driven by deep emotional backstories

The most famous line in anime is widely considered to be Monkey D. Luffy’s aspirational goal "I'm going to be King of the Pirates!" (One Piece), alongside iconic catchphrases like Naruto Uzumaki’s "Believe it!" (Naruto) and All Might's "Plus Ultra!" (My Hero Academia).
